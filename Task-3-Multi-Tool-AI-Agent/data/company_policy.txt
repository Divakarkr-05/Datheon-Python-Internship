Datheon Security & Remote Work Policy (2026)

Section 1: Working Hours & Leave Allowance
- Standard workweek consists of 40 hours (Monday through Friday).
- Annual paid time off (PTO): 24 days per calendar year.
- Sick leave: 10 days per calendar year.
- Public holidays: 12 days per calendar year.

Section 2: Equipment & Reimbursements
- Home office setup stipend: $1,200 USD one-time allowance.
- Monthly broadband internet subsidy: $75 USD per month.
- Hardware refresh cycle: Every 24 months.

Section 3: AI Code Assistance & Tool Usage Guidelines
- All internal development must adhere to sandboxed execution boundaries.
- External API keys must be stored strictly in local environment variables (.env) and never hardcoded in source control.
- Automated testing coverage threshold for Python modules is 80%.
